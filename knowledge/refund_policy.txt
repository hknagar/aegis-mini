Acme Retail Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the purchase date.

Products must be returned in their original condition with all accessories included.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned product has been inspected.

Damaged products caused by normal manufacturing defects may qualify for a replacement or refund after inspection.

Items damaged through customer misuse are not eligible for a standard refund.

Refund processing normally takes 5 to 7 business days after approval.
